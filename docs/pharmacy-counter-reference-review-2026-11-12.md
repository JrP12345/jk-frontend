## Checkout simplification — 12 November 2026

- Completion no longer inserts a success Alert above the counter. A compact last-bill action in the existing bill strip opens the saved bill and receipts. Errors and uncertain commands remain visible until resolved.
- Product filters and lookup mode share one horizontally scrollable strip. Chips do not wrap or shrink; keyboard focus reveals offscreen filters. The loading skeleton uses the same geometry.
- Recorded selects an existing branch customer and links their identity to the bill. Cash, UPI and Card settle the bill; Credit saves an unpaid bill against that customer. Anonymous walk-ins cannot use Credit. This view does not fabricate account balances or create a customer implicitly.
- Cart quantities and discounts calculate locally in integer paise, using the scoped encrypted IndexedDB working set. Last checked batch prices are preferred, with catalogue retail prices available for a provisional display when no batch price is cached. Missing display prices can be read once. Ordinary quantity edits do not request new quotes. Per-row `est.` suffixes are replaced with one clear final-validation note; provisional values never supply issue assertions.
- Save and Save & print each obtain fresh server evidence. Changed totals require another explicit review/Save. Entered cash is preserved when prices change; digital references are cleared so approval must be verified for the new amount. Issuance still checks current prices, stock, units, customer and issuer evidence inside the existing transaction. Adding to a cart does not reserve stock.
- The chosen payment is confirmed once by Save, using the received cash or verified UPI/card reference. Cash requires sufficient tender and an active authorized drawer; digital payments require a verified reference. Normal checkout saves the invoice and records this payment without another collection dialog.
- Invoice and collection remain separate durable commands. A collection failure retains the saved invoice and its frozen collection key/input; recovery/retry does not issue stock again. Corrections and split/partial settlement remain available through the existing collection editor, embedded in the counter for recovery and retained in Sales & collections. Reload/reconnect recovery never treats a remembered preference as permission for a new collection.
- Save & print reads and validates the saved invoice and uses the shared isolated document printer. Printer failure offers print-only retry; it never repeats a sale or collection. Zero-value and Credit bills do not create cash receipts.
- The public offline catalogue module is regenerated from the same source. Offline preparation remains a draft workflow; final issue and payment still require the server.

Focused verification for this refinement: a 102-case run across counter, collections, till, saved-document recovery, payment validation and catalogue passed. A four-case changed-price/recovery rerun includes the additional cash-preservation/digital-reference edge case; seven device-journal/offline-asset checks also passed. Source TypeScript, changed-file ESLint and the payment-boundary check passed. Browser fixtures verify local quantity totals with zero quote calls, fresh validation on Save, one invoice/collection, no second collection dialog, horizontal filters, encrypted reload restoration, and desktop/mobile layouts. Artifacts: `.next/pharmacy-checkout-save-review/` and `.next/pharmacy-checkout-save-review-1366/`. These are intercepted synthetic financial responses; physical printers and live payment providers were not exercised.
